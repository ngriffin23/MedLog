MedLog

Keep track of your medicines, doses and symptoms in one place.

Open MedLog

Free. No account or sign-up.

What you can do

  • Tick off each dose as taken or skipped, and fix the time if it was different.
  • Log symptoms and see which medicines you took in the hours before.
  • Add a note to any dose, such as whether you took it with food.
  • Look back over a day, a week or a month.
  • Copy a summary to show your doctor.

Add it to your iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. From now on, open MedLog from its icon on your Home Screen.

Your entries stay with you

Everything you enter is saved only on your own phone. There are no accounts, and nobody else can see your medicines or symptoms, including whoever shared this page with you.

  • Use the Home Screen icon every time. The icon and Safari keep separate saved entries.
  • Entries don’t move to a new phone, so you’d start fresh there.
  • Reminders only work while MedLog is open. Set a phone alarm for any dose you can’t miss.

MedLog is a tracking tool and doesn’t give medical advice. If a symptom feels serious or sudden, contact a doctor or emergency services.
